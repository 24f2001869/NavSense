## 1. Intended Publication Route

The author will publish strictly via the **conventional / subscription publication route** (hybrid model standard access), **NOT Gold Open Access**.

Under Cambridge University Press publishing policies for hybrid journals such as *The Journal of Navigation*:
* **Conventional / Subscription Publication**: There are **no mandatory author-facing submission fees, page charges, or article processing charges (APCs)**. The article is published online and in print behind the standard subscription / institutional access library system.
* **Gold Open Access (Voluntary Option)**: Authors who elect to make their article immediately free to read under a Creative Commons license incur an Article Processing Charge (APC) (typically £2,000–£2,500+ unless covered by an institutional transformative agreement).
* **Decision**: Because the author does not have grant funding to cover an APC and University of Hyderabad may not have a Cambridge transformative agreement covering 100% of APCs for this journal, the **conventional route must be chosen at all stages**.

## 2. Official Cambridge Fee Evidence

1. **Submission Charges**: Cambridge University Press and ScholarOne do **not** charge any fee to submit, peer-review, or process a manuscript for *The Journal of Navigation*.
2. **Standard Publication Charges**: Under Cambridge's hybrid journal model, standard (non-Gold OA) publication incurs **zero page charges** for research articles within normal length guidelines ($\le 20$ pages).
3. **Colour Charges**:
   * **Online Colour**: Colour figures are published **free of charge** in the online electronic edition (Cambridge Core).
   * **Print Colour**: Authors may optionally pay for figures to appear in colour in the physical printed journal issues. However, print colour is entirely **optional**. By opting for greyscale reproduction in print, **zero colour charges** are incurred.
4. **Author Services**: Cambridge offers optional third-party language editing, translation, and figure preparation services. These are strictly commercial, third-party add-ons and are **not required** for peer review or acceptance.

## 5. Pre-Submission and Pre-Acceptance Verification Protocol

Before final acceptance and production sign-off, the author must manually verify:
1. In ScholarOne: Verify that the manuscript is registered under the standard subscription track.
2. If ScholarOne displays an automated query regarding "Institutional Transformative Agreements", do not commit to any personal payment. If University of Hyderabad does not appear on Cambridge's fully covered list, select the conventional publishing option.
3. Upon receiving the copyright / licensing email from Cambridge (`@cambridge.org`), inspect the license type. Sign the **Standard License to Publish (Exclusive License)** for conventional publication. Never sign a CC-BY agreement that triggers an invoice.
4. When reviewing PDF proofs, ensure the order form indicates: *"Print figures in black and white; online in colour. No offprints required."*
