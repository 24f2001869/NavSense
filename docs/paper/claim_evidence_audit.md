# Claim-by-Claim Evidence Audit

This document provides a formal, forensic audit of every major empirical and theoretical claim made across the research project. Each claim is evaluated against authoritative primary data files, exact denominators, statistical units, and evidentiary confidence tiers.

**Repository URL:** [https://github.com/24f2001869/SIH26168-IDR](https://github.com/24f2001869/SIH26168-IDR)

---

## Evidentiary Tier Definitions

* **Tier A (Directly Demonstrated)**: Empirically verified through controlled, reproducible experiments on primary data files with defined denominators.
* **Tier B (Strongly Supported Interpretation)**: Strongly indicated by empirical data, backed by physical equations, but subject to specific boundary conditions.
* **Tier C (Plausible Hypothesis)**: Scientifically coherent explanation consistent with observations, but unproven or not directly isolated.
* **Tier D (Unsupported / Prohibited as Fact)**: Disproven empirically, mathematically unobservable, unsupported by data, or an overclaim.
