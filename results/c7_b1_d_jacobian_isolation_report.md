## 1. Executive Summary & Primary Hypothesis Verdict

In Stage C7-B1, soft longitudinal acceleration consistency ($a_{\text{soft}} = 3.0\text{ m/s}^2$) successfully reduced along-track position error by **$-36.1\%$** ($284.47\text{ m} \to 181.76\text{ m}$) on continuous highway `Vta04`, but caused cross-track lateral error to expand from $51.53\text{ m} \to 227.64\text{ m}$, creating a $+10.6\%$ net regression in total 30-s drift ($294.27\text{ m} \to 325.60\text{ m}$).

The forensic audit revealed that the acceleration measurement update was correcting attitude states, which subsequently interacted with Non-Holonomic Constraints (NHC). To isolate the causal pathway, Stage **C7-B1-D** froze all parameters, thresholds, initializations, and outage windows, changing **strictly the mathematical structure of the acceleration update**.

### Primary Hypothesis Tested:
> *Does removing the attitude pathway preserve longitudinal improvement while reducing cross-track degradation?*

### The Empirical Verdict:
**YES — but with a profound mathematical qualification:**
1. **Setting $H_\theta = \mathbf{0}_{1 \times 3}$ ALONE is INSUFFICIENT:**
   In coupled mode ($a_x^v \leftrightarrow v_x^v$), setting the direct measurement sensitivity $H_\theta = \mathbf{0}$ leaves the attitude Kalman gain virtually unchanged ($\|K_\theta\| = 5.705 \times 10^{-3} \to 5.707 \times 10^{-3}$) and cumulative absolute yaw corrections unchanged ($66.87^\circ \to 66.62^\circ$). The update leaks into attitude through the **off-diagonal error cross-covariance $\mathbf{P}_{\theta v}$** generated by the INS prediction dynamics ($\dot{\delta \mathbf{v}} = -\mathbf{C}_v^n [\mathbf{f}^v]_\times \delta \boldsymbol{\theta}$).
2. **Strict Attitude Isolation ($K_\theta = \mathbf{0}$) Eliminates the Regression and Unlocks Total Drift Reduction:**
   When attitude is strictly isolated from the acceleration update ($K_\theta = \mathbf{0}$):
   - **Highway Total Drift (30 s)** drops from **$294.27\text{ m} \to 224.66\text{ m}$ ($-23.7\%$ net improvement over C0 Baseline)**, eliminating the previous regression!
   - **Along-Track Error** is cut in half: **$284.47\text{ m} \to 144.64\text{ m}$ ($-49.2\%$)**.
   - **Cross-Track Error** is reduced from $227.64\text{ m}$ down to **$138.41\text{ m}$ ($-39.2\%$ reduction in lateral penalty)**.
   - **Velocity Error** drops from $17.09\text{ m/s} \to \mathbf{13.14\text{ m/s}}$.
   - **Final Heading Error** is cut nearly in half: $59.58^\circ \to \mathbf{32.28^\circ}$.
   - **Suburban Performance (`Vta02`)** preserves its strong gain: 30-s drift improves from $388.10\text{ m} \to \mathbf{293.45\text{ m}}$ ($-24.4\%$).

## 2. Mathematical Structure & State Observability Breakdown

### 2.1 State Representation & Measurement Model
The 15-state ESKF error vector is partitioned as:
$$\delta \mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} \delta \mathbf{p}_{3 \times 1} & \delta \mathbf{v}_{3 \times 1} & \delta \boldsymbol{\theta}_{3 \times 1} & \delta \mathbf{b}_{a, 3 \times 1} & \delta \mathbf{b}_{g, 3 \times 1} \end{bmatrix}^T \in \mathbb{R}^{15}$$

The longitudinal acceleration pseudo-measurement is:
$$z_a = a_x^v = \mathbf{e}_1^T \left( \mathbf{f}_{\text{meas}}^v - \mathbf{b}_a - \mathbf{C}_n^v \mathbf{g}^n \right)$$

### 2.2 Jacobian Formulation
| State Block | State Indices | Nominal Coupled $H$ | Decoupled $H_\theta = \mathbf{0}$ | Strict Attitude Freeze |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Position $\delta \mathbf{p}$** | `0:3` | $[0, 0, 0]$ | $[0, 0, 0]$ | $[0, 0, 0]$ |
| **Velocity $\delta \mathbf{v}$** | `3:6` | $\mathbf{e}_1^T \mathbf{C}_n^v / \Delta t$ | $\mathbf{e}_1^T \mathbf{C}_n^v / \Delta t$ | $\mathbf{e}_1^T \mathbf{C}_n^v / \Delta t$ |
| **Attitude $\delta \boldsymbol{\theta}$** | `6:9` | $[0, g_z^v, -g_y^v] \approx [0, -9.81, 0]$ | $[0, 0, 0]$ | $[0, 0, 0]$ |
| **Accel Bias $\delta \mathbf{b}_a$** | `9:12` | $[-1, 0, 0]$ | $[-1, 0, 0]$ | $[-1, 0, 0]$ |
| **Gyro Bias $\delta \mathbf{b}_g$** | `12:15` | $[0, 0, 0]$ | $[0, 0, 0]$ | $[0, 0, 0]$ |

### 2.3 Why $H_\theta = \mathbf{0}$ Does Not Decouple Attitude
The Kalman gain is:
$$\mathbf{K} = \mathbf{P} \mathbf{H}^T \mathbf{S}^{-1} \in \mathbb{R}^{15 \times 1}, \quad \mathbf{S} = \mathbf{H} \mathbf{P} \mathbf{H}^T + R_k$$

Expanding the attitude rows ($i \in \{6, 7, 8\}$):
$$\mathbf{K}_\theta = \frac{1}{\mathbf{S}} \left[ \underbrace{\mathbf{P}_{\theta v} \mathbf{H}_v^T}_{\text{Indirect Cross-Covariance Pathway}} + \underbrace{\mathbf{P}_{\theta \theta} \mathbf{H}_\theta^T}_{\text{Direct Jacobian Pathway}} + \underbrace{\mathbf{P}_{\theta b_a} \mathbf{H}_{b_a}^T}_{\text{Bias Cross-Covariance}} \right]$$

- In `B1_3_Coupled`: Direct term $\mathbf{P}_{\theta \theta} \mathbf{H}_\theta^T \sim 10^{-5}$, while cross-term $\mathbf{P}_{\theta v} \mathbf{H}_v^T \sim 5.7 \times 10^{-3}$.
- In `B1_3_Decoupled_Htheta`: $\mathbf{H}_\theta = \mathbf{0} \implies \mathbf{P}_{\theta \theta} \mathbf{H}_\theta^T = \mathbf{0}$. However, $\mathbf{P}_{\theta v} \mathbf{H}_v^T$ remains fully active!
- Therefore, **$99.9\%$ of the attitude injection came from velocity cross-covariance $\mathbf{P}_{\theta v}$, not direct $H_\theta$ sensitivity!**
- In `B1_3_Strict_Freeze`: $\mathbf{K}_\theta \equiv \mathbf{0}_{3 \times 1}$ explicitly severs both the direct and indirect pathways.

## 5. Detailed Forensic Analysis & Mechanism Resolution

### 5.1 Clarification on Metric Definitions
Per user methodological directive, two attitude metrics are rigorously distinguished:
1. **Cumulative absolute attitude correction:** $\sum_k |\delta \psi_k|$. This represents the total integrated high-frequency adjustment injected by the constraint updates. In coupled mode, this reached $66.87^\circ$ at 30 s and $124.25^\circ$ at 60 s.
2. **Net attitude change:** $\Delta \psi_{\text{net}} = |\psi_{\text{end}} - \psi_{\text{init}}|$. At 30 s on `Vta04`, the net yaw rotation was $21.15^\circ$ under B1_3 vs $49.96^\circ$ under C0.
The previous report's phrasing ("it is massively rotating the vehicle attitude") was methodologically imprecise. The correct interpretation is:
> **"The acceleration update produces substantial attitude-state corrections, and in several tested windows these corrections materially alter the final heading trajectory."**

### 5.2 Why the Error Moved and Where It Settled
- In nominal coupled B1_3, the cross-covariance $\mathbf{P}_{\theta v}$ pushed acceleration residuals into high-frequency attitude jitter ($\sum |\delta\psi| = 66.87^\circ$). Because the vehicle body frame was continuously perturbed, the Non-Holonomic Constraint (which forces lateral velocity $v_y^v = 0$ in the estimated body frame) constantly applied lateral velocity impulses into the navigation frame. This caused cross-track error to explode from $51.53\text{ m} \to 227.64\text{ m}$.
- When attitude was strictly isolated ($K_\theta = \mathbf{0}$):
  - Attitude jitter was completely eliminated ($\sum |\delta\psi| = 0.00^\circ$).
  - Cross-track error contracted from $227.64\text{ m} \to 138.41\text{ m}$ ($-39.2\%$).
  - **Audit Insight:** The audit demonstrates that, in the tested configuration, removing attitude-state corrections substantially reduces the cross-track degradation associated with the acceleration update.
  - **Remaining Cross-Track Error ($138.41\text{ m}$):** In continuous highway driving, when longitudinal velocity $v_x^v$ is pulled back to realistic values, the NHC Jacobian term $H_{\text{NHC}}[0, 8] = -v_x^v$ is altered. Unobservable yaw-gyro bias ($b_{g,z}$) integration over continuous highway curves represents **a plausible contributor consistent with the observed heading error and cross-track growth** (geometric intuition: $\sim 15^\circ$ heading offset over 1 km travel yields $\Delta y \approx 1000 \cdot \sin(15^\circ) \approx 250\text{ m}$), though its exact quantitative attribution remains to be established in Stage C7-B1-E.
- **Long-Horizon Boundary (60 s):**
  At 60 s on `Vta04`, drift under Strict Freeze is $571.92\text{ m}$ vs $532.94\text{ m}$ in C0. While Strict Freeze prevents the catastrophic blowup of Coupled B1_3 ($659.15\text{ m}$, cross-track $549.82\text{ m}$), the fact that $571.92\text{ m} > 532.94\text{ m}$ demonstrates that **removing the acceleration $\to$ attitude pathway is not sufficient to solve long-duration drift**. Long-duration drift during prolonged GNSS outages is dominated by unobservable motion, heading, and IMU errors.

### 5.3 Oracle Interpretation Correction
Oracle-B1 results must not be interpreted as proving that all remaining error is recoverable acceleration information.
> **"The reference acceleration signal contains information that can reduce along-track error under the tested update formulation. It does not prove that all of the remaining difference between smartphone and oracle performance is recoverable acceleration information, because the oracle update itself interacts with the same ESKF/NHC structure."**

## 6. Strict Epistemic Classification

### 🟢 WHAT WE KNOW (Empirically Confirmed)
1. **Setting $H_\theta = \mathbf{0}$ alone does not decouple attitude** because the off-diagonal covariance block $\mathbf{P}_{\theta v}$ transmits $99.9\%$ of the velocity update into attitude corrections ($\|K_\theta\| \approx 5.7 \times 10^{-3}$).
2. **Strict attitude freezing ($K_\theta = \mathbf{0}$) collapses attitude jitter to zero** ($\sum |\delta\psi| = 0.00^\circ$).
3. **Strict attitude freezing preserves longitudinal drift suppression**: Along-track error on `Vta04` drops by **$-49.2\%$** ($284.47\text{ m} \to 144.64\text{ m}$ at 30 s).
4. **Strict attitude freezing substantially mitigates cross-track degradation**: Cross-track error drops by **$-39.2\%$** ($227.64\text{ m} \to 138.41\text{ m}$ at 30 s) and by **$-64.5\%$** at 60 s ($549.82\text{ m} \to 195.46\text{ m}$).
5. **Highway total drift regression is completely reversed into a net win at 30 s**: 30-s total drift on `Vta04` improves from **$294.27\text{ m} \to 224.66\text{ m}$ ($-23.7\%$)**.
6. **Suburban performance remains stable**: 30-s drift on `Vta02` is **$293.45\text{ m}$ vs $388.10\text{ m}$ ($-24.4\%$)**.
7. **Long-duration blackout drift remains unsolved**: At 60 s, `B1_3_Strict_Freeze` ($571.92\text{ m}$) does not outperform C0 ($532.94\text{ m}$), demonstrating that isolating the acceleration update does not resolve unobservable long-horizon errors.

### 🟡 WHAT WE THINK (Strongly Supported Hypotheses)
1. Unobservable yaw gyro bias integration ($b_{g,z}$) over continuous highway driving is a plausible contributor consistent with the observed heading error and cross-track growth, to be directly tested in C7-B1-E.
2. This particular acceleration pseudo-measurement, with its modeling assumptions and uncertainty, was able to induce undesirable attitude corrections through the existing covariance structure.

### 🔴 WHAT WE DON'T KNOW (Unresolved Boundaries)
1. Whether yaw gyro bias is actually the dominant source of the remaining cross-track error.
2. Whether strict $K_\theta = \mathbf{0}$ is covariance-consistent across general operational conditions.
3. Whether a dedicated lateral kinematic consistency model ($a_y \approx v_x \omega_z$, Stage C7-B2) can safely recover heading information from the smartphone IMU without noise amplification.
